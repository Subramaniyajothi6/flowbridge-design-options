Getting started

Build and publish your first workflow in four steps.

1. Create your workspace

Sign up with your work email. Your workspace holds your workflows, connected apps and team.

2. Start a workflow

Pick a template, or open a blank canvas and add a trigger: the event that starts the workflow.

3. Add and connect steps

Add steps from the panel on the left, then draw a line from one to the next. Open a step to fill in its settings.

4. Test, then publish

Click Test workflow to try it with sample data. When it works, click Publish to make it live.
