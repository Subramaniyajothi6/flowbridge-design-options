Colour palettes that suit orange

Six palettes built around an orange brand. Each shows the colours, a small preview of FlowBridge in that palette, and measured contrast. In every one, orange is kept for actions, and success, warning, error and running each have their own colour.

1. Ember (current)

Current

Orange with warm sand neutrals and espresso dark. Cosy and consistent, but every neutral is warm, so orange has little to stand out against.
